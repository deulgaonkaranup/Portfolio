About Me

I’ve spent years making computers do what they’re told. Now I’m curious about computers that seem to have opinions of their own. Exploring AI through first principles, I like understanding what happens under the hood, from transformer architectures to RAG pipelines, rather than taking the magic at face value. My approach? Question assumptions, experiment with ideas, and build things to see what actually works. Less hype, more engineering.

Professionally, I’ve worked on distributed systems, large-scale data processing, and performance optimization. With a background in computer science and computational intelligence, I’m now applying that engineering mindset to understanding and building AI-powered applications.
